Better Food, Less Waste

UMAMI BOX is designed to make home cooking easier while reducing unnecessary waste. From portioned ingredients to reusable packaging ideas, sustainability is part of how we want the business to grow.

Low Carbon

We aim to reduce food waste by providing portioned ingredients, so customers only receive what they need for each meal.

Recycling Drop-Off

Customers can return reusable containers or packaging materials through our future recycling drop-off system.

Local Suppliers

Where possible, we aim to source fresh ingredients from local farmers and suppliers to support the community.

Reusable Ice Packs

We encourage customers to return ice packs with their next order, helping us reuse materials and reduce packaging waste.

Our Aim

We want UMAMI BOX to offer convenient Asian-inspired food while encouraging more thoughtful food habits, better portion control and less single-use waste.
