About UMAMI BOX

UMAMI BOX was created from a simple idea: making Chinese, Japanese and Korean-inspired food easier to enjoy at home without needing to buy too many ingredients or make every sauce from scratch.

Our Story

The idea behind UMAMI BOX started with the love of Asian food and the challenge of cooking it at home. Many dishes need specific sauces, fresh ingredients and preparation time, which can make everyday cooking feel difficult.

UMAMI BOX brings together meal kits, ready meals and signature sauces to make cooking more convenient while keeping strong, authentic-inspired flavour at the centre of the experience.

Our aim is to offer a practical food service that helps people save time, reduce waste and discover new meals from Chinese, Japanese and Korean cuisines.
